Last updated October 28, 2026

Short version: Tiptoe Tanks does not collect any data about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. It does not use the internet while you play. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (your current run, Medals, upgrades) and your settings (sound, vibration, text size, tips) are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Links in the app

The About screen has links to this website. If you tap one, the page opens in your browser. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we don't share them with anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if crash reporting is added), this page will be updated before the new version is released, and the date above will change.

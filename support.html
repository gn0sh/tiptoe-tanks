Common questions

How do I learn to play?
Open Settings and tap "Replay the training duel". A coach walks you through cards, fuel, attacks and the cannon.

What is the noise meter?
Every card makes some noise. Your meter fills as you play loud cards and settles down a little each turn. If it fills up, the sleeper in the room wakes up, and the player who woke it pays for it.

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. Quitting at any moment keeps your run exactly where it was. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and there is no cloud backup.

Does the game cost anything?
No. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Can I make the card text bigger?
Yes: Settings, then "Card text: large". You can also press and hold any card to see it full size.
